# My Favorite Series: *Arcane*

## Overview

**Arcane** is an animated series produced by **Fortiche Production** and based on the world of *League of Legends*. It premiered in **November 2021** and has earned widespread critical acclaim for its storytelling, animation style, and character depth.

> "You're stronger than you think you are."
> — *Vander*

---

## Main Characters

- **Vi** — A fierce, determined fighter who will do anything for her family.
- *Jinx* — Vi's younger sister; creative, brilliant, but deeply troubled.
- **Caitlyn Kiramman** — A skilled marksman from Piltover who joins Vi.
- *Silco* — A ruthless visionary seeking independence for Zaun.
